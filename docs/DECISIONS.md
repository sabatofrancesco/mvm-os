# MVM Packaging — Decisions

## Brand

- Brand: **MVM Packaging**
- Domain: `mvmpackaging.com`
- Payoff: **Primary Packaging for Beauty Brands**
- Positioning: **Primary Packaging Partner**
- Brand character: elegance, competence and discreet luxury
- Target market: premium skincare and beauty brands

## Commercial model

- Initial target: small and mid-sized beauty brands, with priority on companies up to €25M turnover
- Reference MOQ: **5,000 pcs**
- Reference lead time: **3 months**
- Samples: verify case by case
- Product selection may include items sourced from multiple manufacturers
- Source manufacturer must always be tracked at product level

## Product strategy

- Initial focus: Lip Balm
- Scope expanded to the wider primary-packaging catalogue
- Main catalogue families include Lip Balm, Stick/Panstick, Lip Gloss, Mascara, Eyeliner, Lipstick & Stylo, Tottle, Pen, Jar, Bottle, Compact & Palette and related formats
- Grioni is source manufacturer for applicable lip-balm products
- MYC is a commercial/product source where applicable

## Data governance

- **Notion is the live operational source of truth** for products, CRM, suppliers, marketing and roadmap
- **GitHub is the version-controlled source** for website code, documentation, schemas, exports and automation definitions
- Public-product references are deduplicated by product reference
- Unknown technical values must remain blank rather than being invented
- Standard defaults for catalogue enrichment:
  - MOQ: 5,000
  - Lead time: 3 months
  - Samples: To verify
  - PA / SSH / TD / TS: false until documents are available

## Brand asset rule

The approved geometry of the **V** in the MVM logo must not be modified without explicit approval.
