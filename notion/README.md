# Notion

Notion is the **operational control centre** for MVM Packaging.

## Main areas

- Products
- CRM & Prospects
- Suppliers
- Marketing & LinkedIn
- Website
- Tasks & Roadmap
- Document Library

## Responsibility split

### Notion
Live operational and commercial information: product records, prospects, suppliers, marketing calendar, tasks and document status.

### GitHub
Version-controlled code, documentation, schemas, exports, prompts and automation definitions.

### Google Drive
Binary/product documentation such as PA, SSH, TD, TS, images, presentations and other working documents when connected.

### n8n
Automation and synchronization between systems.

## Synchronization principle

Do not maintain conflicting master records in multiple systems. Notion is authoritative for live product/commercial data; GitHub stores controlled snapshots and the logic used to process that data.
