# Content

Source content for MVM Packaging website, LinkedIn and commercial communication.

## Editorial pillars

- Materials and sustainability
- Beauty-packaging trends and use cases
- Product focus: lip balm, airless, sticks and customization
- Packaging development process and behind-the-scenes expertise (without confidential information)
- Technical/commercial education for emerging beauty brands

## LinkedIn cadence

Reference plan: **2 posts per week**, building authority before and during the website launch.

## Content principles

- Premium but accessible tone
- Technically credible
- No unsupported sustainability claims
- No disclosure of confidential manufacturer/client information
- MVM should communicate as a knowledgeable primary-packaging partner, not merely a catalogue reseller
